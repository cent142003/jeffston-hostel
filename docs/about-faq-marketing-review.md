# About and FAQ publication review

Decision: APPROVED for publication, 21 November 2026. Reviewer: Codex applying Marketing Psychology Lab v1.0.0 criteria; this is a skill-based review, not an independent human endorsement or evidence of conversion uplift.

Decision problem: Students and parents need to assess practical fit and understand booking terms before comparing rooms or contacting reception. The observed barrier is repeated About copy and thirteen ungrouped questions, not demonstrated lack of motivation. No conversion baseline is available.

Models: Jobs to Be Done (compare a suitable student base), cognitive load/Hick's law (scan meaningful groups), and Fogg Behavior Model (clear low-effort next steps).

| Observed fact | Hypothesis/model | Mechanism and implementation | Measurable test | Risks/alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| About repeated facilities and lengthy copy | Concise practical priorities may help comprehension; JTBD | Three highlights plus original controlled video, Compare rooms and Arrange a viewing | Ask students to identify room features and next step; qualified enquiries per eligible visitor | Actual room price or commute may matter more than copy |
| Thirteen questions occupied one list | Topic groups may reduce scanning effort; cognitive load | Three labelled groups; native closed disclosures; complete answers retained | Task completion finding refund terms and semester rules, time and comprehension | Closed disclosures could hide important terms; non-refundable policy also remains before payment |
| Visitors may need clarification | A visible relevant action may ease progress; Fogg | Contact reception help panel; direct contact section | Qualified room/viewing enquiries per eligible exposure, not just clicks | Staff response quality and availability may dominate outcomes |

Ranked approaches by effort/impact hypothesis: (1) retain complete answers and group them, low effort/high clarity; (2) concise About highlights and existing tour, moderate effort/high relevance; (3) future moderated student/parent comprehension pilot, moderate effort/unknown commercial effect. The first two are implemented. A/B testing is deferred until sample sizes support it; no new tracking or personal data collection was added.

Stitch provided the structure. Its unverified lockable storage, sub-metering, utility and transit details were excluded. No new testimonials, safety guarantees, free-service promises, scarcity or response times were introduced. All thirteen approved answers remain identical to migration/faqs.json and FAQ structured data, including non-refundable payments and unconfirmed deposit requirements. No new data fields or messaging integrations.

Validation: 38 automated checks passed (including FAQ schema/visible answer parity, no missing or duplicate questions, collapsed defaults, video controls/no autoplay and existing booking/contact contracts).
