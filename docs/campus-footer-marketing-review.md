# Campus Access and footer publication review

Decision: APPROVED, 21 November 2026. Reviewer: Codex applying Marketing Psychology Lab v1.0.0 criteria. This is a skill-based review, not an independent human endorsement or a measured uplift claim.

Decision problem: Students and parents need to assess commute fit and find dependable navigation/contact details before booking. Observed barriers: repetitive campus cards with mixed campus/local-area content, and no homepage footer. No conversion baseline or user interview evidence is available.

Models selected: Jobs to Be Done (assess a practical campus routine), cognitive load/Hick's law (clear guide choices and grouped footer links), Fogg Behavior Model (easy relevant enquiry and navigation).

| Observed fact | Hypothesis/model | Mechanism and implementation | Measurable test | Risk or alternative explanation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Four mixed campus/local cards repeated facilities | Separate campus choices may ease comparison; cognitive load | Three consistent campus cards, separate Adenta guide and journey checklist | Students find their campus guide and explain what to verify before booking | Real traffic, budget and lecture timetable may dominate choice |
| No footer in source homepage | Closing navigation may reduce search effort; Fogg | Semantic footer, labelled navigation, direct reception links, back-to-top | Successful navigation/contact task completion and qualified enquiries per eligible visitor | Existing fixed action bar already serves some visitors |
| Commute and booking inclusions are uncertain | Practical fit matters; JTBD | Explicit route/traffic/charges checks and non-refundable policy reminder | Comprehension interviews and proportion of qualified enquiries with campus/period details | Reception response quality may be a stronger operational bottleneck |

Ranked approaches (effort/impact hypotheses): (1) consistent factual campus guide labels, low effort/high clarity; (2) footer contact/navigation with visible booking reminder, low-to-moderate effort/high usability; (3) future moderated campus-journey comprehension pilot, moderate effort/unknown commercial effect. First two implemented. No invented commute times, routes, affiliation, testimonials, neighbourhood conveniences or transport guarantees from Stitch's draft were published.

Measurement: no new tracking added. When adequate consent and baseline exist, compare qualified viewing/room enquiries divided by eligible visits, alongside navigation success and comprehension of commute uncertainty and refund policy. Treat clicks as intermediate signals. No fabricated uplift or causal inference.

Verification: 40 automated checks pass, including every guide document, footer anchors/privacy target, contact details, refund terms and preserved existing booking/contact/media contracts. Browser widths 320, 390 and 1280px have no horizontal overflow. Phone footer links minimum44px; campus links minimum48px; mobile footer reserves112px for fixed booking bar. Three campus columns and four footer columns desktop, single column phone. Legon guide and Room enquiry privacy clicked successfully. Back-to-top navigates #home and scrollY0 after fixing a desktop floating-WhatsApp overlap. No real messages, bookings or payments submitted. Browser viewport emulation is not physical handset testing.
