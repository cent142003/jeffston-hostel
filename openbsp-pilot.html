Test WhatsApp with OpenBSP

This page is an isolated trial for Jeffston Court Hostel. It does not connect accounts, store phone numbers, book rooms, collect payments, or send messages itself.

1. Connect OpenBSP

  1. Sign in to the OpenBSP free dashboard with Google or GitHub.
  2. Choose Integrations → WhatsApp and connect a test WhatsApp Business number you control through the official Meta option, if eligible.
  3. Keep the OpenBSP inbox open. Do not connect the live hostel number for this experiment.

2. Open a test conversation

International format. Digits only, or start with +. This number stays in your browser.

Important: WhatsApp will open a prepared message. You must press Send there yourself. No message is sent automatically.

3. Check that it arrived

  1. Send the prepared message manually from a different phone/account you control, not the same number receiving it.
  2. Confirm exactly one incoming message appears in the OpenBSP inbox.
  3. Reply manually from OpenBSP, then confirm the reply arrives once on the test phone.
  4. Do not enable AI auto-replies until the manual test passes.
No guest information, website analytics, webhook credentials or payments are used in this pilot. This page is not part of the live booking flow.
